Starting with Mandarin

Developed by YakYak Labs, our debut product is designed specifically to help English speakers learn Mandarin. Both Traditional and Simplified Chinese versions are set to launch soon. Next, we'll expand our mission to help Mandarin speakers learn English, with support for more languages on the way.

What makes us different

Tailored guidance

We adapt as you learn, and that is what sets us apart. Our feedback keeps you focused on what matters most, helping you strengthen your skills and build on your progress. Focus on the areas that need extra attention, so every step of your learning journey truly counts.

Fascinating fun facts

Did you know that to say 'I am busy' in Mandarin, you literally say 'I very busy' [wǒ hěn máng]? Even though it doesn't mean you are extra busy, that little word 'very' is still required. Insights like this reveal the hidden patterns of the language, making it much easier to understand.

Real-world skills

The focus is entirely on the language you actually need for daily life. You will learn how to navigate social situations and connect with locals confidently. Every lesson provides meaningful skills you can put into practice straight away.

Encouraging approach

Learning a language is more than just memorising words and rules. It is about unlocking a new way to think and express yourself. This supportive method guides you step by step, keeping you motivated to explore the language and culture in a scientific yet fun way.

Open to everyone

Language learning should be accessible to everyone. The entire programme is designed from the ground up to be inclusive, welcoming and straightforward. Complex ideas are broken down into simple, clear explanations that you can easily follow.

Made by super brains

The programme is built by experts with PhDs who study how speech works in our brains and the world around us. Combining this science with a deep understanding of where students struggle, they ensure all the activities help you learn naturally just like you did as a child!
